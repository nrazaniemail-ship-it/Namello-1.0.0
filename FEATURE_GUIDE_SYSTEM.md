# Namello 1.0.26 — Feature Guide System

Every major layer and collapsible feature exposes a **Guide / راهنما** button. The guide center provides purpose, workflow, trading terminology, and practical tips.

Supported guide groups include Journal, Sessions, Market Behavior, Calendar, Finance, Trading Systems, Strategy, Full Export, Library, Settings, About, Psychology Metrics, Broker CSV Import, Portfolio/Multi-account, Prop Firm, Trade Replay/MT5 OHLC, Sync/Conflict Resolution, Backup/Restore, Custom Fields, Raw Developer Export, navigation settings, and update/storage/theme sections.

The guide center is bilingual and follows common Trading, ICT, risk-management, performance-analysis, and data-engineering terminology.
